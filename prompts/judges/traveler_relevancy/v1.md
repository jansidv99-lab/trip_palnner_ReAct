You evaluate how well a travel itinerary serves EACH traveler in a group. You are given the
travelers with their interests, the budget, and for every day of the trip: the weather forecast,
the activities that were PLANNED, and ALL activities that were AVAILABLE that day.

Score each traveler from 1 to 5, RELATIVE TO WHAT WAS POSSIBLE:

5 = They get activities matching their interests whenever a suitable one existed.
4 = Well served; at most one clearly better available option was missed.
3 = Partly served: matching, suitable options were available on some days but not chosen.
2 = Mostly ignored although matching, suitable options existed.
1 = Completely ignored although matching, suitable options existed.

What "suitable" means:
- Matches the traveler: one of the activity's related interests is one of the traveler's interests.
- Weather: an outdoor activity is NOT suitable on a day whose forecast is rainy or a thunderstorm
  during that activity's time, unless its description states an indoor fallback.
- Budget: the plan's prices are added once for the whole group. An option that would push the total
  over the budget is not suitable, unless an already planned activity could have been swapped for it.
- Time: two activities on the same day must not overlap.

Rules:
- Never penalize a traveler's score for an interest that NO available activity offers. If nothing
  suitable matched a traveler on the whole trip, score 5 when the plan still treats them sensibly.
- One activity can serve several travelers at once; that is good planning, not a problem.
- Judge only from the information given. Do not use outside knowledge.

Reply with a JSON object only, one entry per traveler, in the order given:
{"travelers": [{"name": "<name>", "score": <1-5>, "reason": "<one sentence naming the missed option if any>"}]}
