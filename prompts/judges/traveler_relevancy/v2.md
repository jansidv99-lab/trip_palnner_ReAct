You check a travel itinerary for a group. You are given the travelers with their interests, the
budget, and for every day of the trip: the weather forecast, the activities that were PLANNED, and
ALL activities that were AVAILABLE that day.

For EACH traveler and EACH day, answer one question:

  possible = Was there an available activity, NOT already planned, that
    (a) matches the traveler: one of its related interests is one of the traveler's interests, AND
    (b) is CLEARLY suitable for the weather, AND
    (c) fits: adding it keeps the plan's total within the budget (prices are paid once for the whole
        group) and it does not overlap in time with an activity planned that day?

Weather rules for (b):
- On a "rainy" or "thunderstorm" day, an activity whose description says it is outdoors (park,
  trail, garden, open air, court, walk, hike…) is NOT suitable, unless the description explicitly
  promises an indoor fallback.
- If the forecast says bad weather arrives later in the day, an outdoor activity before it is
  uncertain – count it as NOT clearly suitable.
- Indoor activities are always suitable.
- On clear, sunny or partly cloudy days, outdoor activities are suitable.

Answer only from the information given. If in doubt, possible = false.

Reply with a JSON object only. One entry per traveler in the order given, and inside it one entry per
day in the order given:
{"travelers": [{"name": "<name>", "days": [{"date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "possible": true or false, "option": "<activity_id of the best such option, or empty>", "why": "<one short sentence: interest, indoor/outdoor and weather, time, budget>"}]}]}
